openMX front end · concept

Drive it: ↑↓ move · ←→ change · Enter ✕ · Esc ◯ back · Q/E L1/R1 tabs · P pause. Click works too.

Kept from the original

  • The lit orange backdrop, the dark column, the logo top left.
  • The maroon pill with the white rim and the hard drop shadow, now drawn as vectors so it stays sharp at 4K.
  • The slanted page title over the black band, and the angled tab that holds the button hints.

Settings, regrouped

  • 16 rows on one long page become 5 tabs: Ride, Display, Audio, Controls, Camera. Switch with L1 and R1.
  • Every row is a value you can change in place. A side panel names the option, says what it does and lists every choice.
  • The separate Game Audio Options page becomes the Audio tab. The pause's Audio row opens straight to it.
  • Rider Name moves to the Garage, beside the number it gets painted with. Overlay is called HUD, and Graphics is called Draw Distance.

New places for the new things

  • Mods gets its own spot on the main menu, with packs you can switch on and off, replacing the single On/Off row.
  • Free Ride lists the disc's tracks and the mod tracks under headings, with a ride setup strip: machine, wrecks, modifiers.
  • Modifiers sit in the Ride tab and in the ride setup, the place you think about them.
  • Every per-player page (Garage, Controls, Camera, Modifiers) has the same Player row at the top.
