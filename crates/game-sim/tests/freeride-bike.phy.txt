# A bike, hand-written for the tests — NOT the disc's.
#
# `.phy` is the game's own parameter format and `phy::Phy::read_bytes` is the ported loader, so a test
# bike is written in it. Nothing in this file came off a disc: it is a plausible 250 cc four-stroke
# and no number in it is claimed to be the game's. The keys here are the ones `ride.rs` actually
# drives; the loader's defaults cover the rest of the file.
#
# Units are the simulation's: feet, slugs, pounds of force, g = 32.2 ft/s^2. The four per-surface
# grip keys — `WheelFriction`, `WheelDrag`, `MaxDriveForce`, `MaxSideForce` and `SideForce` — are
# coefficients *per unit of tire load*, not pounds, so they are small numbers here where they used
# to be hundreds. A wheel of this bike carries about two hundred pounds, which is the factor.
#
# `BodyTireForcePosition` is where the spin step puts the tire's force on the chassis, a short way
# under the centre of gravity as on the original's own bikes, not where the wheel hangs: the hubs are
# `game_sim::stock::MOUNTS`. The `Skid*` and `Slipping*` keys are what a sliding, spinning or locked
# tire still pushes with; without them it pushes nothing at all (S58).
#
# Read by crates/game-sim/tests/ride.rs and crates/game-sim/tests/replay.rs.
