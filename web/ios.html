openMX for iPhone & iPad

Add FIZL0's app source to your sideloading app once, then install openMX from it and get every update there. You sign it with your own certificate, so it installs only on your devices.

The same source has every FIZL0 app, so one add covers them all. Nothing happens when you tap? Open this page in Safari on the iPhone with the app installed, or copy the URL and add it under Sources in the app.

Put your disc on the phone

  1. Start openMX once, so its folder appears.
  2. Open the Files app → On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → openMX.
  3. Put your own MX Unleashed (USA, SLUS-20767) disc image there: AirDrop or save the .iso to Files, then move it into the openMX folder. No game data is included.
  4. Start openMX again. Touch controls, or pair a controller in Settings → Bluetooth.
