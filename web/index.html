About

The original

MX Unleashed, Rainbow Studios' motocross game, published by THQ in 2004: free roam over big open maps, supercross and nationals, the biplane, the monster truck and the rest of the lot.

PlayStation 2Xbox2004

openMX

A from-scratch rewrite in Rust. The physics, the timing and the rules are ported and proved against the original, tick for tick; the picture is new, drawn as sharp as your screen allows. Everything is read from your disc as you play.

Desktop & mobile

Linux & Steam Deck

The same game as a native build: the GPU renderer, pads, split screen and mods. Unpack it, put kit.toml beside it with your disc image's path, and run openmx.
