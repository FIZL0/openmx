What is in it

Browser, PC and Steam Deck

Plays in a web browser with nothing to install, or as a native build for Linux and the Steam Deck. A Windows build is on the way. Pads, keyboard, any resolution, widescreen, and a frame rate as high as your screen's.

Online

Ride with friends over the internet or your LAN: send a room code, or quickplay into an open lobby. Rollback netcode keeps every rider in step, up to sixteen seats, browser and desktop together.

The original's ride

The bike physics, the 24 tricks, landings, wrecks and the biplane are ported from MX Unleashed and checked against it in an emulator, tick for tick. Free roam, nationals and supercross on the disc's own maps.

Split screen

Up to eight riders on one screen, each with their own pad, bike, rider and controls, in the original's two-player layouts or quarters and eighths.

Mods

Track and music mods, upscaled texture packs and gameplay modifiers. Turn them off and the ride is the original's again, exactly.

MX vs. ATV Unleashed tracks

Tracks from MX vs. ATV Unleashed and MX vs. ATV Untamed import as mods from your own copies of those games, and ride with MX Unleashed's bikes.

Questions

Do I need the game?

Yes: your own disc. openMX has none of MX Unleashed's data. It reads the tracks, bikes, rider, textures, menus and sound from an .iso you make of your own PlayStation 2 disc (the US release, SLUS-20767), on your own machine. Nothing from the disc is on this site or uploaded anywhere.

Can I play MX Unleashed on PC?

Yes. openMX is a remaster of MX Unleashed that runs in a web browser and natively on Linux and the Steam Deck, with Windows coming. It isn't an emulator: the game is rewritten, so it runs at any resolution, in widescreen and at a high frame rate, with a pad or the keyboard.

Is it the same game as the original?

On the bike, yes. The physics, tricks, wrecks, biplane and timing are ported from MX Unleashed and proved against it in an emulator, so the same inputs ride out the same way. The renderer and the menus are new.

Is this a decompilation?

It's a rewrite. The original's code was studied to port its behaviour, function by function, into new Rust; none of the original's code or data is in openMX. That is also why it needs your disc.

Is this MX vs. ATV Unleashed remastered?

No, openMX remasters MX Unleashed, the 2004 game before it. But MX vs. ATV Unleashed's tracks import as mods from your own copy, and ride in openMX.

Can I play online?

Yes, in the browser and on the desktop: host a lobby and send its code, join one, or quickplay. Rollback netcode keeps the ride smooth, and a handshake makes sure every machine rides the same build.

Does it cost anything?

No. openMX is free and open source, on GitHub. It is a fan project, not affiliated with THQ, Rainbow Studios or Sony.
