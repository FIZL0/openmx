- **An early look at MX vs. ATV Unleashed's five cars** (buggy, monster truck, baja, golf cart and sandrail), driven with that game's own physics. They are an addition, off by default, and need your own copy of *MX vs. ATV Unleashed* (PS2): build the cars pack from it with [openmx-mods](https://github.com/FIZL0/openmx-mods)' `import-atv cars`, put it in the game's `mods/machines/` folder and turn Read the Mods Folder on. There is no Garage row for them yet: add a line such as `mxvatv_car = buggy` (or `monstertruck`, `baja`, `golfcart`, `sandrail`) to `settings.txt` beside your disc, then take the car with Switch To Machine on the pause menu. While it's on, Game Modifiers has an MX vs. ATV Cars category for top speed, acceleration, grip and suspension. For now the cars are invisible and silent, and they're offline only. Drawing them, their sound and a Garage row come in a later release.
- openMX now runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later): open the .dmg from the release page and drag openMX onto Applications.
- MX vs. ATV's quad, cars, biplane and Kiowa: the chase camera follows each of them the way MX vs. ATV does, on that machine's own camera settings from its pack.
- MX vs. ATV's quad: its rider takes that game's own quad poses and plays its stunt moves in the air.
