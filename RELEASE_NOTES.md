- Shadows on All no longer adds the baked hill shadows from 0.2.4; the hills look as they did before
- iPhone/iPad: the game now fills the whole screen — the picture and the menus' backgrounds run under the Dynamic Island, the HUD and menus keep clear of the island but not of the home bar, and the home bar fades away while you play.
- iPhone/iPad: a Frame Limit of 60 (or 30) now runs the screen at that rate, so a 120 Hz iPhone no longer judders at 60 fps.
