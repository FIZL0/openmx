<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- iPhone and iPad: music plays again. When another app's audio is playing, the game's music now holds as PAUSED instead of going silent while the menu says it is playing. Press play on the music (Play Music in Settings → Audio, or the pause menu's music player) and the game's music takes over.
- Supercross stadiums and the nationals have their dirt textures and ground detail again, on every platform. They had been drawn in one flat dirt colour.
- Races no longer show free ride's challenge markers (the hit event on Los Angeles and other tracks): a race loads none of free ride's events, radar icons or zones.
- iPhone and iPad: smoother riding. Each picture is now timed to the screen's refresh, which fixes a stutter where the bike and camera moved in uneven steps.
- Still missing from races: the other riders on the grid and the gate dropping on screen. They come in the next release.
