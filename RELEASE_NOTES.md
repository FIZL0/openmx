- The first release of openMX. Ride MX Unleashed's free-ride worlds and race tracks with the original's bike, rider, tricks and wrecks, ported from the game and checked tick for tick against it, plus the biplane and the other machines from the pause menu's Switch To Machine.
- Play in the browser at openmx.dev (nothing to install, phones and tablets included, with touch controls), or download it for Linux and the Steam Deck. The desktop build updates itself when a new release is out.
- Ride together: split screen on one machine, or online with a room code or over your local network.
- Mods: extra tracks, trick packs and music, from the mods folder or the in-game mod store.
- iPhone and iPad: add openMX to Feather, AltStore or SideStore in one tap at openmx.dev/ios.html, then install it and get every update from the app.
- Bring your own disc: openMX reads your own MX Unleashed (USA) disc image on your device. No game data is included or uploaded.
