- iPhone/iPad: no longer stuck at 30 fps; the game now runs up to the screen's own rate (60, or 120 on ProMotion) when the phone keeps up.
- Mod store: texture packs — a Texture Packs tab, a Pack Order page, and uploading your own pack.
- The two MX Unleashed worlds are now built straight from your disc.
- A single track's radar no longer shows a map name; only worlds name their maps.
