<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- The Garage has a Fliers tab: pick your plane and helicopter from every installed machine pack (MX Unleashed's own, and MX vs. ATV's Pitts, Spitfire, Kiowa and Little Bird with the fliers pack), with no Fliers setting to turn on. Your old Fliers and Flier Skin settings carry over. Previews, skin buttons and the mid-ride picker come next.
- The pilot now sits in the Spitfire's and the Little Bird's seat instead of poking out underneath.
- MX vs. ATV's biplane wrecks and recovers as in that game.
- Phones and tablets: the speedometer sits top right, right under the FPS counter (or in its place when Show FPS is off).
- Phones and tablets: the Resume Ride offer is gone.
- Phones and tablets: a controller you connect always takes over player 1 from the touch pad, mid-ride too.
- Taking a player's controller away on the Controllers screen now takes that player out of the ride.
