<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- The mod store carries every kind of mod: machine packs and handling mods install and upload there too, each checked before it installs
- MXA Track Studio tracks: drop an .mxatrack file in mods/tracks and ride it (new, and not yet tried on many tracks)
- Props Put Back (Game Modifiers, off by default): knocked-over props go back when you reset or cross the start line
- A car you drive now hits and pushes the parked cars, alone and in split screen; a switched car restarts from its own safe spot
- Split screen: AI riders, a flown plane and thrown riders meet the parked cars, and a downed rider's bike strikes the other riders' bikes
- Online riders' bodies move as they do offline
- Phone apps: landscape only, and the screen stays on while anything downloads
- iPhone Safari: rides on maps with no roads no longer break
- Big worlds no longer stall for a moment when you come back from the pause
