<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- Races have other riders: a one-player race puts AI riders in every other lane of the grid (pick how many in Race Setup). They wait behind the gate with you, collide with you, and are counted in your place, the lap ranking and the results board. They are not drawn on screen yet, and after the drop they don't follow the track's racing line yet. Both come in a later release.
- The start gate drops on screen: when the countdown ends, the gate falls forward on every Supercross and National track.
- The pause menu has a Garage: change machines from a page of tiles with previews, without leaving the ride. Triangle swaps back to the machine you rode last, and the pause shows a LAST MACHINE box beside Now Playing. The Garage's Machines tab is now a grid of tiles too.
- MX World on iPhone and iPad: fewer hitches. Coming back from the app switcher and jumping to a marker no longer freeze the ride for a moment.
- MX Unleashed: WORLD has its tracks spread more evenly, without the long empty stretches at the world's edges.
- MX vs. ATV's quad (the addition) has its own chase camera, engine sound and tire dirt, and is drawn with working suspension, wheels and handlebars and its rider on it.
- In MX World, the trucks, knockable props and moving toys of every track are solid now: you can hit them and knock them over.
- The Air Wrecks help says what it does: with Air Wrecks off, a high nose-first landing is ridden out.
