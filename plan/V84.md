## The lookup, as opentennis's

Each texture is taken from the first match:

0. **Texture packs**: mod folders under `mods/` whose `mod.json` says `"kind": "textures"`, with
   key-named files under `textures/`; stacked, a pack nobody ordered on top, settings.txt
   `texture_packs = a, b` / `texture_packs_off = c`.
1. **Key-named files** in `mods/texture-replacements/` (`<name>-<key>.png`, any subfolder or flat,
   matched by the part after the last `-`), any size, straight alpha; `OPENMX_TEXTURE_RELOAD=1` reloads
   changed files live.
2. **A PCSX2 texture pack** (hash-named PNGs at that folder's top level): port texhash.rs so a pack made
   for the original MX Unleashed in PCSX2 works as is. If the GS upload of this game's textures does not
   match texhash's assumptions (mips, CLUT layout), say so in the journal and leave 2 for a follow-up
   rather than guess.
3. **A mod's own upscales**: `mods/<kind>/<id>/textures/upscaled/<archive stem>/<name>.png` for textures
   the mod itself carries (below).
4. The disc (or the mod's own texture, for a mod).

## Dumping, and the key

`openmx --dump-textures` writes every disc texture at its native size to `mods/textures-src/` in readable
folders (`<archive>/<member>/<name>-<key>.png`): tracks' ground pages and props, bikes, riders, the
biplane, HUD and menus. The key is stable across runs and builds: a hash of the decoded texture (as
opentennis's key is), not its order in an archive.

## Mods

openmx's mods carry their own PS2 textures: a track mod (`mods/tracks/<id>/`, openmx-mods'
`standard/OPENMX-TRACK-STANDARD.md`) has `.pst`/`.psm` members, trick packs and future bike mods
likewise. `upscale_mods.py` walks every `mod.json` under the roots, as opentennis's does, but instead of
a `.glb`'s embedded images it decodes each `.pst` texture the mod carries (with the port's own decoder,
through a small `openmx --dump-mod-textures <dir>` or a game-render bin, not a Python re-implementation)
and writes `textures/upscaled/<pst stem>/<name>.png` beside the mod, with `sources.json`. `"upscale":
false` in mod.json skips a mod. A world (MD8) draws each tile's mod upscales.

## The setting

Graphics tab: **Upscaled Textures**, the master switch for all of 0–3, a P126 departure that the Original
Game switch turns off. Off, the game draws exactly the disc's textures and the replay tests are untouched
(AGENT.md). opentennis B222: a tiled quad or tiled ground keeps its 1× tiling — the UVs are not scaled by
the replacement's size. Mips are rebuilt from the replacement. Works in the GPU renderer; say in the row
what the software renderer does.

## Not shipped

Like everything from the disc, upscales are made on the user's machine and never committed or shipped;
`mods/textures-src/` and `mods/texture-replacements/` are git-ignored. chaiNNer is not installed on this
machine today (opentennis's script defaults to `~/.local/bin/chainner-app/chainner`): the tests must not
need it.

## Proving it

crates/openmx tests:
- a key-named PNG in a scratch `mods/texture-replacements/` is drawn in place of its disc texture with
  the setting on, not with it off, and a tiled one keeps its tiling;
- a texture pack mod beats `texture-replacements/`, and settings.txt's order and off list work;
- a track mod's `textures/upscaled/` PNG replaces its `.pst` texture in that track;
- `--dump-textures` writes the same key for the same texture on two runs.

tools: `python3 tools/upscale_mods.py --check` over a fixture mod lists its missing textures and exits 1;
with stand-in PNGs in place (no chaiNNer) it lists nothing and exits 0.
