# MD21 — MX vs. ATV's trick clips play on the rider and the bike

User, 2026-10-23: "put the trick animation as top priority". Under Tricks MX vs. ATV (MD16), every
trick the pack adds is ridden with no pose of its own, so on screen nothing shows one happened. That
covers the eight extras, the two 360s and the front flip. The ride log says so:
`tricks: no clips from … (its .mot files are not read)`, in `trick_packs.rs::missing_clips`.

## Why nothing plays

The pack carries the clips: 141 rider clips under `mods/tricks/mxvatv_tricks/Rider/Anim/`, and the
bike's under `Veh/Anim/` (171 `.mot` files in all). `crates/game-render/src/skin.rs`'s `Clip::parse`
reads only version 3, which is every `.mot` on MX Unleashed's disc. MX vs. ATV's start `f9 ff ff ff`,
which is version 7 (the MD16 journal says v6/v7). So the parse refuses them.

## What to do

1. Find the v7 layout from MX vs. ATV's own `.mot` reader in its decompilation (openmx-mods has it;
   `notes/tricks.md` names the anim ids). Don't guess from the bytes. Extend `Clip::parse` to read
   v6/v7 into the same `Clip`. v3 has to come out byte-for-byte the same, so MX Unleashed's clips
   are untouched.
2. Bind them to MX Unleashed's rider and bike skeletons. Channels are bound by name (`Clip::joints`).
   Check MX vs. ATV's names against MXU's (the first rider clip's first channel is `Pelvis`). List
   any channel that doesn't bind, and say what that does to the pose.
3. Load the clips. `Set::member` should look in the pack folder for `<P>_rider_<name>` and
   `<P>bike_<name>`, the way MX vs. ATV's loader names them (notes/tricks.md:57). This applies to the
   extras (`Trick::EXTRA + k`), `threesixtyL`/`threesixtyR` on the 360's 35° clip state, and the
   front flip. The front flip has no clip in either game: pick one (the backflip's, run so it turns
   forward, or the airborne lean as today), and say why in the journal.
4. Drop or narrow `missing_clips`'s line so it names only what still has no clip.
5. Check by eye against MX vs. ATV in PCSX2 if a disc and state are at hand. If they're not, use
   `./kit shot`-style frames of the port at a few points through each trick, and put that in "Not
   verified".

## Proof

- game-render test: every `.mot` in a pack built by `import-tricks mxvatv` parses. The test skips
  when there's no pack, and says it skipped. Every MXU disc clip still parses to the same `Clip`.
- openmx/game-render test: a ride on the pack that does Cross-up in the air poses the rider off
  `9oclockindianair`'s clip, not the airborne lean. A 360 plays `threesixtyR`.
- MX Unleashed's replays and the original-tricks rides are unchanged.
