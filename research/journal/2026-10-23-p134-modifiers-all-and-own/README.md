2026-10-23. Task: `./kit plan P134`.

Game Modifiers now has two ways in. The host's page has All Players at the head of its Player row:
a knob set there is every seat's, and a knob whose seats differ shows Mixed. Every other player
opens the page from the pause their own pad opened (or, online, from their pause or their lobby)
and sets only their own seat; the world's knobs are shown but are the host's. Online, a guest's own
change and the host's All go through P100's agreed tick, and the lobby gathers and shows each
seat's set. Per-seat online is limited to rides started from the lobby, and online the biplane
knobs are the host's (see 1-…-FINAL.md).
