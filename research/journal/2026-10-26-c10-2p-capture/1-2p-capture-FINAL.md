## What the original does

### The 2p state ticks at 60 Hz, one tape field a tick

The plan's "12,918 frames ≈ 6,459 ticks" was wrong. `dt` is 1/60 (0.016667 / 0.0167 alternating) on
every row, and `research/c10/align.py` matches each row's polled pads (`pad0_a`/`pad1_a`) to tape field
`tick − 127882` (offset 0/−1 the whole way, 200-row blocks). The tape's 12,917 fields are the ticks
127882..140798; the playback pauses at the end. (Saved as a memory: other 2p captures size `-n` from the
field count.)

### The playback is one ride, on either build

- The v2.9.127 playbacks (NominalScalar 0.05, 0.1 and 0.25) agree on every wreck, ragdoll, trick and clip
  stretch to within one tick; rows differ in the low bits (`rng_state` from 127895, positions by ~0.02 ft)
  because the recorder reads a row at one of three phases of the tick (see the memory on capture-row
  phases), not because the ride differs.
- v2.9.52 (`research/c10/check.toml`, a 36-column spec at NominalScalar 0.5, `research/c10/compare.py 2p
  check-v2952`): the same 17 wrecks for the same reasons at the same ticks (±1), both riders' chassis
  centres within 5 ft until the first put-back that lands a tick apart, closest rider-rider 136.7 ft
  (129844), closest rider-car 184.1 ft — as on v2.9.127.

### What the ride holds — and does not

The user's note expects wrecks, collisions, the ragdoll, player-vs-player collision, riding the trucks,
wreck sensitivity and the animations. This playback holds wrecks (17), ragdolls (17), world contacts
(4, all scenery: a body ray striking the static world), near misses, tricks, backflips, reverse, foot-down and
put-backs. It holds **no rider-vs-rider contact, no truck ride and no prop contact**: on every row of
the v2.9.127 captures every collision-record entry's `other` (+0x74: split.toml's "the other side's
collision record, 0 = scenery") is 0 and both wheels' path-car word (+0x1f4, S136) is 0
(`research/c10/owners.py` lists every value they take: only 0); the riders are never closer than 136 ft,
and no rider comes within 184 ft of a path car. The v2.9.52 playback (positions and the wheels' path-car
words only) shows the same distances and no wheel on a car.

## How it was proved

- A PINE recording compared frame by frame: the capture of record (12,917 rows), a second full v2.9.127
  playback, and a v2.9.52 playback, compared by `research/c10/compare.py` and the two indexes' diff.
- `research/c10/align.py`: the tape-to-tick mapping, over all 12,917 rows.
- `crates/game-sim/tests/two_p.rs` (8 tests) on `context/capture/2p/2p.csv`: row count and contiguity,
  dt, both lanes' guards on every row, both pads live, both riders ride, both bodies posed; every wreck,
  ragdoll, put-back, world contact, near miss, trick, backflip, clip stretch and lean extreme at its
  indexed tick; no rider/car/prop contact. `./kit check -- -p game-sim --test two_p`: 8 tests passed; with `ROWS` raised to 12,918 the row test failed ("rows left: 12917 right: 12918"), so the test reads the data, then it was put back.

## Not verified / not 1:1

- **The user's described rider-vs-rider collisions and truck rides are not in the playback.** Both PCSX2
  builds (v2.9.127 and the recording's v2.9.52) play the tape into the same ride, which never brings
  the riders within 136 ft or a rider within 184 ft of a path car. Either the user's live session
  parted from what its .p2m2 replays (input recordings replay pads from a state, and anything not in
  the state — the live session's timing, a setting — can part them), or those moments were in a
  different session. Unblocking S158 (rider-vs-rider) and S159 (trucks) needs a new 2p recording that
  holds them, checked by playback before it is handed over. S157's crates, signs, beach ball and trees
  are likewise not in it: the 4 world contacts are scenery body rays.
- **Which bike and rider each player has**: the bike and gear colours are by eye from one screenshot. Which of 500_01 / 500_02 and which named rider profile (Troy Adams, Ronnie Renner) belongs to which player is not mapped: only P1's `thor_helmet_01` pointer was followed.
- **The near-miss index is my reading of the wreck test**, not the game's own branch trace: g clauses
  read the tick's peak g (`g_peak`), attitudes the chassis Euler angles, and I take the airborne pitch and
  roll limits as judged on a touch. P1 at 131484 touches down with |roll| 85.95° — past the 85° limit — and
  rides out (that tick is also P1's scenery contact): either the limit is judged on another attitude
  than `ch_euler`, or not on that touch. A finding for S156, not resolved here.
- **The landing crossup**: wrecks 2/3 (P1 132997, 139659; P2 140363) are attributed to the crossup clause
  by elimination (hang time past `crossup_wreck_time`); the crossup angle itself is not a captured word.
- **The wreck clause per wreck** is inferred from the reason and the captured quantities in the order of
  air.rs's `wreck_test_in`, not traced in the original.
- **`car%d_vel` and `car%d_clock` are not what they were taken for**: the velocity word reads 0 and the
  clock does not move. A car's velocity is (xform row 3 − prev) × 60 (S136's virtual); the index derives
  it so. The columns stay so the capture keeps its layout.
- **Rows are read at one of three phases of a tick** (an onset can sit a tick early or late against
  another playback); the test pins this capture's ticks, and another playback's index can differ by one.
