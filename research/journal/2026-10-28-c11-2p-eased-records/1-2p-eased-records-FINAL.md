## What the original does

- **The two eased axes are the port's `Lag`, bit for bit.** For each riding tick (no gap before it),
  I stepped from the original's value on the row before, using the previous row's dt (a 2p row's dt is
  the next frame's, which the ratio column confirms: `ratio = dt/tau` reads the row before's dt) and
  the request's target (+0x174 mix, +0x170 phase), with a snap when held:

  | rider, axis | riding ticks | bit for bit (value and ratio) | torn rows | unexplained |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | P1 mix | 10,983 | 10,947 | 29 | 7 |
  | P1 phase | 10,983 | 10,921 | 43 | 19 |
  | P2 mix | 11,179 | 11,055 | 114 | 10 |
  | P2 phase | 11,179 | 10,852 | 291 | 36 |

  - "Bit for bit" counts a tick when the target of this row or of the row before gives the original's
    value. P2 splits between the two because its lane is read on the other side of its body's step
    (V90). With this row's target alone, P2 reaches 10,138 / 8,269; with the row before's alone,
    10,338 / 9,357.
  - "Torn" means one of three things:
    - the record did not move (the row was read before the step);
    - it moved two steps;
    - the target implied by the ratio sits between two rows' targets. For example, P2 128136 implies
      0.6337 between rows of 0.6004 and 0.6671: the request was written twice between two reads.
  - The unexplained rows cluster at the frames where the recorder struggled, and they fall on the same
    frames for both riders: 129629, 129882, 129894, 129903, 129943 sit around the gaps at 129905..129991,
    and also 132953/132962. A tear of the whole row is the likely cause, not either lane.
  - The time constants are 0.125 / 0.15 on every row of both riders.
- **The blended arms' residue is not the eased axes.** These are V90's replay totals per rider, on ticks
  where the port plays the original's (state, mode):
  - mix bit for bit;
