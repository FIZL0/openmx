# 1 — machine race jumps and path aim (FINAL)

Task S292c. 2026-11-18.

## What I did

- The brief's "helper a card with flag 0x80000 calls" is the race's jump watch, already ported in
  S302 (`RaceRules::jumps`). `LapRules::machine_step` now runs it after the odometer for cards with
  `WATCHES_JUMPS`, its notices as `MachineNotice::Jump`. It borrows a `RaceRules` built around the
  machine race's `LapRules` (only `jump_settle` read, the manager's flags copied back) rather than
  changing `jumps`.
- New `LapRules::machine_path_aim`: the aim every card tells when the manager holds a track and has
  `PATH_STEER` (manager flag 0x800000; the brief is right that it is the manager's, not the card's).
- `machine_tick` / `machine_card` take `jump_settle` and `Option<&mut TrackPaths>`.
- opentennis: no race cards or paths there; nothing taken.

## What the original does

- `./kit dis 0x002642b8 --len 0x20`: card+8 & 0x80000 -> `./kit fn 0x00267d90` (mgr, card, dt).
- `./kit dis 0x00264ba8 0x00264da8`: the path aim, written up in research/oracle.txt (S292c block under
  score_machine_race_tick). It differs from the race's computer aim (score_race_aim): the spot is
  searched afresh every tick (path_spot, every stretch), the node walk has no line-node stop, and the
  way is the path's own way ahead at the node — no look-ahead along the paths.

## How it was proved

- Function oracle: `the_machine_race_matches_the_score_managers_mode_1_handler` (tests/race.rs), the
  whole mode-1 handler on 20,000 generated cases. Now: jump watch at even odds per card (grounded /
  was grounded / to-judge bits each way, `jumps_of` words, settle time from `helper_clock`), manager
  `PATH_STEER` and `JUMP_JUDGED` each way, and on half the cases a generated track (`t_paths`, nodes'
  `+4` bit 0 one time in three, pools sometimes nearly dry) at mgr+0x68. Every listener call (jump
  slots 0x90/0x88 with their 0/1, bests 0x74-0x7c / 0x7c-0x84 with mgr and card), every card word
  including its spot +0x88, aim +0xa0 and jump words, the paths' flags and the pools — bit for bit.
- Floors: every jump notice over 100 times; over 5,000 steered cards, 500 aims walked on, 500 stepped
  back from a last node, 30 one-node paths (aim naming no node), 300 points pulled back.

## Not verified / not 1:1

- A spot naming no node after the path search (a track with no paths, or the search's pool empty on a
  card whose spot named none): the original reads the word at 0x54 of low memory; the port walks no
  further. Not generated by the oracle — it cannot happen with a loaded track (paths always present,
  the search always writes the spot when its pool is not empty at the start).
- No live capture of a machine race with path steering or jumps: the oracle covers the handler with
  generated inputs only (no machine-race image exists; see S339's note).
