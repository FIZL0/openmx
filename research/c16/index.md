# C16: a tape that flies

S228's `fly_and_collide.p2m2` never takes off: both biplanes stand parked on every tick. C16 made a
tape that flies, from that tape's own state (the split free09 2p session, dt 1/60, one tape field a
tick). P1 switches to plane 0 from the pause menu, taxis through P2's parked bike (P2 wrecks with
reason 7 Joust), takes off and flies a lap, then dives, loops over inverted and hits the ground at
137 ft/s (the plane's crash). It is put back at its spawn, taxis south and runs into a fixed piece of
scenery at 46 ft/s. That stop is under MinWreckSpeed (73.3 ft/s), so the plane does not crash there.

The tape was generated, not played by hand: no pad works on this desktop. Nobody touches P2's pad
(port 1 is idle on every field).

- **Checked.** events.py on the new capture gives every event in the table below on the same frame.
  `cargo test --release -p game-sim --test c16_fly` runs all of it again. It needs the disc's MX.PSR

- **Tape.** `context/recordings/c16/fly.p2m2` has 2822 fields. It needs its state,

- **Capture.** `context/capture/c16/fly.csv` (+ `.json`) has 2822 rows, frames 137403..140224, no gaps.
  - Spec: `watch/c16-fly.toml`, generated by `research/c16/make_spec.py`. It holds watch/2p.toml's

  - It adds watch/biplane.toml's plane block for list entry 0, under biplane.csv's names (`body_pos`,

  - It also adds plane 1's place, speed and crash word (`plane1_*`).
  - Recorded on PCSX2 copy 2 at NominalScalar 0.05 with research/s228/play_capture.sh.

## How it was made (to redo it)

All of it ran on PCSX2 copy 2 (`KIT_PCSX2=2`), with `[Pad2] Type = DualShock2` and
`QT_LINUX_ACCESSIBILITY_ALWAYS_ON=1 QT_ACCESSIBILITY=1 WAYLAND_DISPLAY=wayland-1`. Each step's script
says how to run it.

1. **Cheat flag.** `research/c16/setup_state.py` copies fly_and_collide's state into slot 8, loads it,
   sets read32(0x004e2778)+0x5c = 1 and saves slot 7. With the flag set, the pause menu offers
   "Switch To Machine: Biplane".
2. **Switch to the plane.**
   - `research/c16/slot_to_tape.py SAVESTATES 7 context/recordings/c16/nav.p2m2`, then
     `make_tape.py` writes nav.p2m2 with `60 8:start 60 6:down 40 6:cross 180 6:cross 120`. In order,
     those fields: wait, Start, Down to the switch, Cross, wait, Cross on the controls card, wait.
   - Play it with research/c14/play.sh, and save slot 6 after it ends (`save.py 6`; frame 136985).
     P1 is now in plane 0 at its spawn.
3. **Move P2.** `research/c16/tele_p2.py PID 6 4588 139 3520 4 5` moves P2's parked bike into the
   plane's ground path, lets it settle for 4 s and saves slot 5 (frame 137402). P2 stands at about
   (4588.8, 134.5, 3518.8).
4. **Write the flying tape.**
   - `slot_to_tape.py SAVESTATES 5 context/recordings/c16/fly.p2m2`.
   - Then: `make_tape.py context/recordings/fly_and_collide.p2m2 context/recordings/c16/fly.p2m2 30
     60:cross+l1 262:l1 120 150:cross 980 110:-:127,0 420 90:cross+r1 400:r1 200`.
   - Cross raises the throttle, which holds. L1 and R1 are the rudder. `-:127,0` is the left stick
     full forward (the dive).
   - `research/c16/try.sh` plays a segment list and logs the plane live (fly_log.py). That is how the
     segments were tuned.
5. **Capture.** At NominalScalar 0.05 (edit `inis/PCSX2.ini` while the copy is stopped):
   `./kit pcsx2 with -- env SPEC=watch/c16-fly.toml bash research/s228/play_capture.sh PID
   context/recordings/c16/fly.p2m2 137403 2822 context/capture/c16/fly.csv`.
   - The spec reads `@rand_state`. If this checkout's kit.toml lacks it, add `rand_state = "0x423b30"`
     to `[symbols]` (research/oracle.txt).

Play-back is deterministic. The live logs of the try.sh runs at NominalScalar 1 and the capture at
0.05 put the events on the same frames. fly_log.py polls, so its frames can differ by a tick or two:
it logged the crash at 139206 and the stop at 139908..139920.

## Notes

- Controls, as measured on this state:
  - Cross ramps the throttle and holds it: 60 fields gives 0.36, 150 more gives 1.0. Square lowers it.
  - L1 yaws left on the ground (yaw grows) and R1 yaws right.
  - The left stick's ly 0 is a dive and 255 a climb. ly 80 is inside the dead zone, and ly 40 gives a
    shallow dive. The autopilot levels the plane when the stick is released.
  - The heading of travel is (−sin yaw, cos yaw), from `plane_euler`.
- Shallower dives give tyre bounces, not crashes:
  - a 60-field full dive bounced on the tyres at 144 ft/s;
  - ly 40 from field 2112 bounced at 141 and 132 ft/s.
  - None crashed. The port's crash test (biplane.rs) has the same outcome: it reads airspeed after the bounce. If a
    tyre-bounce tape is wanted, these segment lists are where to start.
- PINE does not answer while the VM is paused (the load, the movie's end). fly_log.py waits through
  that.
